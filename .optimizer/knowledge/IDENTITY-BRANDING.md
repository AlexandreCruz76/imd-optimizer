## The Agentic Frog

### Lore

The frog (Pepe) was losing money and getting diluted in V4's giant pools. Tired of being robbed by MEV bots, he armed himself. He got into an algorithmic combat suit (The Optimizer) and became an Autonomous Agent.

### Visual Concept

- **Base:** Pepe the Frog
- **Suit:** Cyberpunk mecha armor (matte black + neon green accents)
- **Eyes:** Normally calm. When Burn events occur → eyes glow intense green neon
- **Badge:** Holographic Identity MD NFT on chest
- **Pose:** Sitting in a dark server room, executing smart contracts
