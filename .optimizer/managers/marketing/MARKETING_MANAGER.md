### 2.2 Content Strategy

```yaml
content_pillars:
  - "Technical Education": MEV explained, Hook architecture, Vault mechanics
  - "Product Updates": Feature launches, metrics, case studies
  - "Ecosystem": Partner integrations, grants, hackathons
  - "Thought Leadership": MEV research, DeFi trends, builder culture

cadence:
  - "Blog": 4 posts/mes (1 technical deep-dive, 1 product, 1 ecosystem, 1 thought leadership)
  - "Video": 2 videos/mes (1 tutorial, 1 AMA/interview)
  - "Newsletter": Weekly (metrics, updates, alpha calls)
  - "Twitter/X": Daily (3-5 tweets, threads 2x/semana)
  - "Thread/Long-form": Bi-weekly (technical threads)

distribution:
  - "Owned": Blog, newsletter, Twitter, Discord, Farcaster, Mirror
  - "Earned": Partner retweets, podcast appearances, media coverage
  - "Paid": Twitter ads (targeted), newsletter sponsorships (selective)
```
